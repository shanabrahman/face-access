Use the camera only to record attendance. No head movement is required; hold still while your face is confirmed.

Attendance uses three consecutive face matches and checks the captured photo with a passive anti-spoof model. This can reject some printed-photo attacks, but does not guarantee protection against replayed video or sophisticated spoofing.

For the 9:00 AM–5:00 PM shift, check-ins before 9:15 AM are on time, 9:15–9:30 AM show an orange warning, and arrivals after 9:30 AM are marked LATE in red with minutes late.
